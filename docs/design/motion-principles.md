# Motion Principles

Status: Living Document

Purpose:
Define how motion should feel across the product.

Motion is not decoration.

Motion is communication.

---

# Core Principle

Every animation must answer one question:

"What changed?"

If motion does not explain change,
remove it.

---

# Motion Should Feel Calm

Animations should never compete with learning.

Users come here to understand Japanese,
not to watch UI.

Motion should disappear into the experience.

---

# Motion is Functional

Good motion should:

• Explain hierarchy
• Explain cause and effect
• Reduce cognitive load
• Reinforce spatial memory

Not entertain.

---

# Preferred Characteristics

Slow enough to understand.

Fast enough to never feel delayed.

Soft.

Predictable.

Interruptible.

---

# Avoid

Large zooms.

Violent bounces.

Long delays.

Continuous movement.

Auto-playing decorative animation.

Anything that steals attention from learning.

---

# Companion Motion

Companion never pops.

Companion arrives.

Fade.

Small slide.

Small scale.

Then silence.

---

# Success

When users remember what happened,
instead of remembering the animation.
