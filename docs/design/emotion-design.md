# Emotion Design

> **Status:** Approved

Purpose:
Define what users should feel while using the product.

We do not design features.

We design emotional trajectories.

---

# Beginning

"I can do this."

Reduce anxiety.

Reduce uncertainty.

Reduce cognitive load.

---

# During Learning

"I'm making progress."

Small wins.

Visible understanding.

No pressure.

---

# Difficult Moments

"I don't have to remember everything."

The system remembers.

The user learns.

---

# After Learning

"I want to come back."

Not *only* because of streaks — Streak is a real, shipped Gamification reinforcement mechanic
(G1–G3, `docs/design/design-reconciliation.md` §3), not banned or devalued here.

Because the experience felt good.

---

# Long-Term

The product should gradually feel familiar.

Like returning to a favorite place.

Not because it demands attention.

Because it quietly earns trust.

---

# Companion

Companion is not friendship.

Companion is presence.

Presence without interruption.

Recognition without performance.

---

# Success

The strongest emotion should never be excitement.

It should be comfort.
